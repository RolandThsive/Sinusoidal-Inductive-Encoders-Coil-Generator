Privacy

The coil generator runs inside your browser. Your settings and your boards stay on your computer: nothing you enter or generate is uploaded.

What The Tool Does Not Do

  • It has no account and no login.
  • It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser's storage.
  • It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • It sends none of your settings, files or results to any server.

Your browser keeps these files in its normal cache, so later visits start faster. Clearing the browser cache removes them.

Share Links

Share makes a link that holds your changed settings after the # sign. Browsers do not send that part of a link to a server: the settings are read by the page on the computer that opens the link. Whoever you give the link to can see those settings.

Import, Export And Downloads

Files you import are read by the page on your computer and go nowhere else. Export and the downloads are made on your computer and saved by your browser.

Report A Problem

Report A Problem opens a new issue on GitHub with your browser's name and the tool's version already written in. Nothing is sent until you press Submit on GitHub, and you can change or delete that text first. Issues on GitHub are public.

When The Tool Is Shown Inside The Blog

The blog page that shows the tool is a Blogger page by Google, with its own cookies and its own privacy policy. That page tells the tool only its own address, so that Share can make a link to it.
