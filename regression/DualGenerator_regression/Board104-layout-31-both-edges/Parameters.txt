WARNINGS OF THIS RUN
--------------------
  ROTOR_1_TO_RX2_MM conflicts with ROTOR_AUTO_EXTENSION_OUTER_MM: ring 1 ends at r = 14.75 mm and set 2's RX copper starts at r = 17.17 mm: 2.42 mm apart, less than ROTOR_1_TO_RX2_MM (3 mm). Shorten ring 1 (ROTOR_AUTO_EXTENSION_OUTER_MM in Dual_Rotor1_Parameters.py), widen TRACK_GAP_MM, or move the RX2 band out
  ROTOR_2_TO_RX1_MM conflicts with ROTOR_AUTO_EXTENSION_INNER_MM: ring 2 starts at r = 16.98 mm and set 1's RX copper ends at r = 15.05 mm: 1.93 mm apart, less than ROTOR_2_TO_RX1_MM (2 mm). The fine target adds a ripple of 16 per turn to the coarse signal. Shorten ring 2 (ROTOR_AUTO_EXTENSION_INNER_MM in Dual_Rotor2_Parameters.py), widen TRACK_GAP_MM, or move the RX1 band in
  ring 2 starts at r = 16.98 mm, over set 1's TX ring (which reaches r = 17.03 mm): the fine target would modulate TX1 16 times per turn. Shorten ring 2's inner extension (ROTOR_AUTO_EXTENSION_INNER_MM in Dual_Rotor2_Parameters.py)
